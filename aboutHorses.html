About

This is a site for sorting Japanese thoroughbred race horses 🐎

Terms

  • JRA: Japanese Racing Association
  • Coat: Color of the horses' fur coat
  • Genders: There are mutliple terms for a horse's gender depending on age. Male/female horses that are 4 year olds and under are called colts/fillies respectively, and called horses/mares when 5 and older. Geldings are neutered male horses. You may be wondering about the term "stallion," which is a term only used for male horses in breeding.
  • Broodmare: Female horses kept for breeding
  • Stud: Male horses kept for breeding
  • Off-Track Thoroughbred (OTTB): A thoroughbred horse that has finished or transitioned away from a racing career
  • Surface: Horse races can take place on various surfaces, which require different strengths to run well on. Some horses have the ability to run on multiple surfaces, or transitioned from surface to another. Some track surfaces are man-made, while others are comprised of natural materials.
